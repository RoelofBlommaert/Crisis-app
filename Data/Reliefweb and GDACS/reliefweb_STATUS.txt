ReliefWeb pull skipped: no RELIEFWEB_APPNAME environment variable set.
ReliefWeb now requires a pre-approved appname (since 1 Nov 2025).
Request one at https://apidoc.reliefweb.int/parameters#appname, then set:
  $env:RELIEFWEB_APPNAME = "your-approved-appname"   (PowerShell)
and re-run this script. GDACS already covers natural-disaster events
without needing a key -- see fetch_gdacs_events.py.
